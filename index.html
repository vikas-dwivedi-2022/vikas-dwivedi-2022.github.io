01 / About

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at CREATIS Biomedical Imaging Laboratory, INSA Lyon, France, working on scientific machine learning for forward and inverse PDE problems, with applications in computational fluid dynamics and medical imaging.

I completed my M.Tech at IIT Delhi and Ph.D. at IIT Madras under the supervision of Prof. Balaji Srinivasan. My background includes over two years in industry as a Data Scientist at Vunet Systems and almost three years of postdoctoral research in India, the United States, and France.

I received the Best PhD Thesis Award in Data Science from IIT Madras.
